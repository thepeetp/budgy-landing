Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Budgy collects when you use the Budgy app, the Budgy LINE Official Account and the public payment pages at pay.budgy.life, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and what you can do about it. It follows the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA) of Thailand. It is general information, not legal advice.

1. Who is responsible and what we collect

Budgy decides why and how your personal data is used, so under the PDPA Budgy is the data controller. Write to privacy@budgy.life about anything in this policy.

What we collect

  • Sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture from the service you sign in with (LINE, Google, Facebook or Apple) and that service's ID for you; or your email address and a password. Some services let you hide your email.
  • Profile: display name, language and currency, and the optional details you add, such as birth date, retirement age, financial goals, risk preference, phone number, PromptPay number and account name, and a profile photo.
  • What you record: transactions, categories, budgets, goals, debts, assets, recurring items, notes, groups, friends and split bills, including names of other people that you type.
  • Photos and files: receipt photos, transfer slips, bank statement PDFs and photos you send to the assistant. We keep them in cloud storage so you can open them again. Slips and statements can show names and account numbers.
  • Tax figures: the tax year, income, tax withheld and allowances that you confirm from a tax document you upload, or ask the assistant to remember. The uploaded file, and the name, address and ID number on it, are not kept (section 3).
  • Photo library (iPhone): if you allow it, the app searches your photo library on the device for transfer slips using on-device text and QR recognition. Photos that match neither slip words nor a QR code are not uploaded. On Android you pick the photos yourself.
  • Assistant: what you write to the assistant in the app or in our LINE Official Account, its replies, and notes it keeps about facts you told it (for example family, work, where you live, preferences and goals). You can ask it to forget a topic. There is no screen to review the notes yet.
  • Subscription: your plan and status, and the store transaction details Apple or Google give us to confirm a purchase. Apple and Google take the payment; your card details never reach us.
  • Usage and device: a random ID the app creates for your installation, with the screens and features you use, the time and the app version; device model, operating system, language and push notification token; crash reports and performance data; and install attribution from AppsFlyer (which campaign brought you, and device identifiers; on iPhone the advertising ID only if you allow tracking).
  • Server logs: our servers record requests to keep the service running. Logs can contain your account ID and email address and are sent to our monitoring provider.
  • Messages to us: support messages, bug reports and feature requests.
  • Consent: your choices about optional uses of your data: which purpose, given or withdrawn, when, which version of this policy was in force, where in the app you chose, the app language, and the IP address and device description (user agent) of the request.

2. Why we use it

  • To provide Budgy: sign you in, keep and show your records, work out budgets and balances, split bills, send the notifications you ask for and check your subscription.
  • To run the AI features you turn on (section 3).
  • To keep Budgy secure, prevent abuse and fix errors.
  • To measure how Budgy is used and how people find it, so we can improve it.
  • If you opt in, to count your spending in statistics about groups of people (section 4).
  • To check how well the assistant and the scanners work: we read samples of AI conversations and request logs, run an automated analysis of conversation samples with Gemini, and may copy excerpts into our issue tracker on GitHub to fix a problem.
  • To answer your messages and to meet legal duties.

Where the PDPA asks for your consent, for example for the AI features, we ask for it separately and you can withdraw it (section 9). Using Budgy does not by itself give that consent.

3. AI features and Google Gemini

Budgy's AI features run on Google Gemini. At the date above, Google is the only AI provider Budgy uses.

What is sent to Gemini

  • The AI assistant (in the app and in our LINE Official Account): the message you write, earlier messages of the conversation, photos you attach and, so that it can answer, the records of yours that the question needs: amounts, dates, categories, item names, budgets, goals, debts, assets, the tax figures you saved, group and friend names, profile details such as birth date and financial goals, and the notes it has kept about you.
  • Scanning: the photo of a receipt or transfer slip, or the pages or text of a bank statement, and for slips the recipient's name read from the slip with your category list. Slips and statements show names and account numbers.
  • Tax documents: the file you upload in Profile, Tax (a PDF or photo of a ภ.ง.ด.90/91, a 50 ทวิ or proof of paying tax). Gemini receives the whole document, which shows your name, address and national ID number as well as income and tax. Budgy asks it for the figures only and rebuilds the reply from a fixed list of numeric fields, so the name, address and ID number are not passed on to the app, saved or logged. The file is not kept, and nothing is saved until you check the figures and confirm.
  • Categories and icons: the name and amount of entries you save without a category, and item names used to pick or draw an icon.
  • Insights and coach: summaries of your spending (amounts, categories, dates, item names, budget and goal progress) to write Home insights, forecasts, goal tips and the weekly coach profile; and finished conversations, to pick out lasting personal notes.
  • Quality review: samples of AI conversations.

Budgy does not put your email address, password or sign-in tokens in AI requests.

How Google handles it

Budgy uses Google's paid Gemini API. Google's published terms for paid use (ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms, read on 29 October 2026) say that Google does not use prompts or responses to improve its products, logs them for a limited time to detect abuse, and may store or cache them in any country where Google or its agents have facilities. Google can change its terms.

Your choice

The app asks before AI features start, and you can change your mind at any time in Profile, Help & Support, AI data sharing. Turning AI off stops the assistant and the receipt, slip, statement and tax document reading in the app from sending your data to Gemini. It does not stop two kinds of background processing on our servers: suggesting categories and icons for entries you save, and writing insights and the coach profile from your records. The list above shows what they send. The switch does not apply to messages you send to our LINE Official Account: the first message creates a Budgy account linked to your LINE ID, and the welcome message points you to this policy before anything else is processed.

AI answers can be wrong. See the Terms of Service.

4. Who receives your data

  • Google: Gemini (section 3), Firebase Authentication, Cloud Messaging for push notifications, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring and Analytics, and Google Sign-In.
  • Amazon Web Services: our servers, database and file storage, in Singapore.
  • Grafana Labs: monitoring of server logs and performance.
  • AppsFlyer: install attribution and a few events (sign-in method, first slip scan, first entry, finishing onboarding, importing a statement, starting a trial or subscribing with the amount and currency). It may pass install and purchase events to the ad platform that ran a campaign.
  • Sentry: error reports, where it is enabled in your version of the app.
  • Apple and Google: Sign in with Apple, App Store and Google Play purchases and subscription checks.
  • LINE and Meta: sign-in, and message delivery for our LINE Official Account.
  • An email provider, which delivers emails such as password resets.
  • GitHub: excerpts of conversations or bug reports when we file an issue to fix a problem.
  • Partners: if you open a partner offer, the partner learns that you came from Budgy through an offer tracking number we add to the link, and Budgy may earn a commission. Budgy does not send partners your spending data.
  • People you share with: a payment link or card you send shows what you put in it (section 5).
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Budgy does not sell your personal data.

Group spending statistics (only if you opt in)

If you switch on group spending statistics, Budgy counts your spending in statistics about groups of people. It is off until you turn it on. Using Budgy does not turn it on.

  • What is counted: your total expenses in baht for each month, for each of Budgy's default categories. A sub-category counts in the default category above it, including a sub-category you created. A main category you created yourself is not counted. Spending in group bills, guest accounts and deleted accounts are not counted.
  • What you see: if you have a Premium subscription and have opted in, the app shows your own spending in a category next to the median (the middle value) of everyone who opted in, for the last full month. A category appears only if at least 20 people who opted in spent in it.
  • What Budgy builds: a report for its own use. For each default category it lists the number of people and the 25th percentile, the median and the 75th percentile of monthly spending in baht. A category appears only if at least 20 people are in it. The report has no user ID, email address, device ID, name or amount of any one person.
  • Who receives it: at the date above, Budgy holds the report and nobody outside Budgy receives it. Budgy may in future share such statistics with businesses, which may be for payment. If it does, they will come only from people who opted in, only for groups of at least 20 people, and we will update this policy before it starts.

These statistics are built from your spending. They describe groups and are not meant to identify you, but we say so here because your spending is what they are made of.

You can switch this off at any time (Profile, Notifications, Group spending statistics). From then on you are left out of every new calculation. Figures already calculated or shared cannot be recalculated to remove you, and switching off does not delete your own records.

5. Public payment links

When a Budgy user creates a payment link for a friend, a page at pay.budgy.life/s/ followed by a random code opens for anyone who holds the link, with no sign-in. It shows the friend's name as the user typed it, the sender's display name, the balance, each bill's title, date, total, number of people and how it was split, and, if the sender saved them, the sender's PromptPay number and account name with a QR code. It does not show the names of other people on a bill.

The page is marked so search engines do not index it, sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts. Budgy counts how often the link is opened and tells the sender whether and when it was. The link stays active until the sender cancels it (Split, the person, link, Cancel link); a cancelled link stops working at once.

If a page shows information about you and you want it removed, ask the person who sent it or write to privacy@budgy.life.

6. Transfers outside Thailand

Budgy's servers, database and file storage are in Singapore. Google, AppsFlyer, Grafana Labs, Sentry, Apple, Meta, LINE and GitHub may process data in other countries, and Google says its Gemini API may store or cache data in any country where it has facilities. The PDPA (section 28) allows a transfer abroad where the destination has adequate protection or an exception applies, such as your consent. When you turn on AI features you consent to the transfers described in section 3.

7. How long we keep data

  • Your account, records, photos and files: while the account exists.
  • AI conversations: while the account exists. They are not deleted automatically today.
  • Notes the assistant keeps about you: until they expire, you ask it to forget them, or the account is erased.
  • AI request logs (what was sent to Gemini outside chat, such as for scanning and categorising, and the model's raw reply): deleted automatically after 90 days. Corrections you report on an AI result: 365 days.
  • Tax documents: the file is not kept. The AI request log holds only the file type and size and the figures read from it, not the file, the name, the address or the ID number, and deletes them after 90 days whether or not you confirm them. The figures you confirm stay in your tax profile while the account exists.
  • Usage events: 24 months, then deleted automatically. They are the record of which screens and features are used and when; they contain no text you typed. They are linked to your account and to the random installation ID the app creates, not to an advertising or device ID.
  • Server logs and monitoring data: held by our monitoring provider under its own settings; Budgy has not set a shorter period.
  • Consent choices: while the account exists. Each choice is added as a new entry, so withdrawing adds an entry and does not overwrite the earlier one.
  • Group spending statistics: calculated from current records each time they are asked for, so you are left out of the next calculation when you switch off or delete your account. Figures already calculated or shared are not recalculated.
  • Purchase records: while the account exists, and longer where tax or accounting law requires.

8. Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile, Help & Support, Delete Account and type DELETE. This closes the account at once: you can no longer sign in, and your email address, your LINE, Google, Facebook and Apple IDs and your phone number are detached from it so they can be used for a new account. The records linked to the account, such as transactions, files, conversations, tax figures, your name and photo, are marked deleted but are not erased from our database by this button.

To have them permanently erased, write to privacy@budgy.life from the address you registered with. We erase them within 30 days. If you signed up with Facebook and remove Budgy in your Facebook settings, Facebook may send us a deletion request, and we then delete the account linked to that Facebook ID.

We may keep what the law requires us to keep, such as purchase records. Usage events follow their own 24-month period.

9. Your rights

Under the PDPA you may:

  • Access your personal data and get a copy (section 30). We answer within 30 days.
  • Receive it in a readable format, and ask us to send it to another controller where that is technically possible (section 31). In the app, Profile has a CSV export of your transactions.
  • Object to its use (section 32).
  • Ask us to erase it, destroy it or make it anonymous (section 33).
  • Ask us to restrict its use (section 34).
  • Have incorrect data corrected (section 35). Most of it you can edit in the app.
  • Withdraw a consent at any time (section 19). Withdrawing does not affect what was done before. In the app, AI data sharing is switched off under Help & Support, and group spending statistics under Profile, Notifications.
  • Complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee (section 73).

Write to privacy@budgy.life to use these rights. Some of them may be limited where the law allows, for example where we must keep a record.

10. Children

Budgy is not intended for users under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Under PDPA section 20, consent from a minor who is not yet of age (and not married) may also need the consent of the person with parental responsibility, and for a child under 10 it is required. If you are a parent or guardian and think a child has given us personal data, write to us.

11. Security, identifiers and changes

Traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS, and our database and file storage are encrypted at rest. No system is completely secure.

The app does not use browser cookies. It keeps your settings and sign-in on your device, and the SDKs listed in section 4 use device identifiers as described there.

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with a new date, and if a change needs your consent we will ask for it again.
